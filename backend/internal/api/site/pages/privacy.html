OrbGuard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ORB GLOBAL LTD, registered in England and Wales under company number 08274220, registered office 7 Bell Yard, London, WC2A 2JR, United Kingdom ("Orb", "we"), handles information in the OrbGuard app. Orb is the data controller for this processing. The current version of this Policy is always available at https://guard.orbai.world/privacy.

1. Information we process.

  • Account data: your email and authentication identifiers, via your shared Orb account. Sign-in options include email link, password, passkey, and Google/Apple.
  • Device & security data: device model, OS version, security posture, app/network signals, and scan results used to detect threats and show your privacy score.
  • Message text you choose to check: OrbGuard never reads your SMS inbox and holds no SMS permission. When you paste a message into the scam-text checker or share one to OrbGuard from another app, that text (and the sender, if you enter it) is transmitted over TLS to Orb servers to analyse it for scams, phishing and malicious links. This may include processing by our AI service providers acting as processors under contract. We use this ONLY to detect and warn you about threats — never to sell, advertise or profile you.
  • Links and QR codes you scan or check are sent to Orb servers to evaluate their reputation and warn you about phishing or malicious destinations.
  • Location data: when you enable Locate or periodic reporting, we process the device's location to show it to you (the account owner) and support anti-theft.
  • Camera images ("thief capture"): when you (or you via the web panel) trigger a photo, the device's front camera captures an image that is uploaded to your account so you can identify who has the device. These images may contain a person's likeness.
  • Payment data: purchases are processed by the app store or payment provider you buy through; we receive confirmation of the purchase and subscription status, never your full card details.
  • Diagnostics: limited logs to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use it

To provide and secure the Service; to run scans, alerts, and anti-theft features you enable; to manage your account and subscription; to prevent abuse; and to comply with law.

3. Your consent and responsibility

You enable location and camera features yourself, and you are responsible for having any consent the law requires from people who use or may be captured by the device. Do not enable these features on devices or people you are not authorized to monitor.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that operate the Service under contract (including payment providers for billing), with your account (the device owner), and as required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Camera images and location history are retained per your settings and can be deleted from your account.

6. Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no system is perfectly secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Exercise them by contacting us at support@orbvpn.com. You may also complain to your data-protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy; material changes will be notified in-app or by email.
