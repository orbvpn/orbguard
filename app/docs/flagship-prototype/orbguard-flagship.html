Close the circle.

The OrbGuard logo is a ring. In this design the ring runs the whole product. Whole means you are covered. A gap means one thing to fix. Segments mean a check is running. Nobody has to learn anything else.

Is this just a mock?

It is a working prototype in HTML, not the Flutter app yet. It is made so the app can match it one for one, and so nothing on it promises what the product cannot do.

True size

Every screen is drawn at 393 by 852 points, the real logical size, then scaled to fit your display.

Your kit

Colors, glass, radii and spacing are copied from brand.dart. The fonts are the three the app already bundles. One lime action per screen.

Your icons

Every icon is a Solar icon already shipped in app/assets/icons. None were drawn for this page.

Your product

Real shields, real checks, real score rules, real prices. Only times, counts and the person are samples.

One shape. Every state.

No red screens and no wall of toggles. The ring changes shape, and that is the whole status system. It is drawn with arcs, gradients and blur only, so in Flutter it is one CustomPainter, not an image.

Same fonts. A wider voice.

Archivo, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono stay, exactly as the kit names them. One thing changes. The Archivo file inside the app already carries a width axis that no screen uses. Headlines now run wide and lighter, so they take the calm, round shape of the ring.

No new font file

Archivo-Variable.ttf in app/assets/fonts already spans width 62 to 125. In Flutter this is one FontVariation('wdth', 118) inside BrandText.

Mono does one job

IBM Plex Mono keeps section labels, times and points, the job brand.dart gives it. Sentences that sat in mono are now IBM Plex Sans.

Other scripts untouched

Persian stays on Vazirmatn and Arabic on IBM Plex Sans Arabic. The width change applies to Latin display type only.

Why it had to change.

What the code and the store screenshots show today. Each number below was counted in the repository, not estimated.

3

names for the same features: guards on Home, shields on Protect, and a third set in the drawer.

16

drawer items behind a hamburger in everyday mode. 34 in Pro mode. This is where services get lost.

0

layout breakpoints. Foldables, tablets and desktops all get the phone column, capped at 760 points.

5 of 11

checks can run on an iPhone, yet the results line says “11 checks ran clean”.

2 of 8

home guards can never read On. Breach monitor and hidden VPN watch have no working probe.

166

lines of user-facing text contain an em dash, the mark that makes copy read as machine written.

0

screens for family or devices, although two of the three plans sell exactly that.

2

home tiles with cut-off names in the store screenshots: “App malware s…” and “Hidden VPN wa…”.

Nothing gets lost.

Every feature keeps working. It just gets one name and one place. The six shields absorb the drawer, Check holds everything you ask on demand, and the expert tools move under Me.

From this page to the app.

What is already in the code, what is new, and what needs wiring before the screens above are true.

Already in the app

  • The whole kit: brand.dart tokens, glass, type, the Solar icons
  • Six shields and the eleven check stages
  • The privacy score engine and its seven rules
  • Scam, link, QR, breach, hidden VPN and secure call checks
  • Find, ring and photograph for a lost phone
  • Three tiers with store prices, shared with OrbVPN and OrbBrowser
  • Scan credits and the rewarded ad
  • Sheets for every pop-up

New to build

  • OrbRing, one CustomPainter that replaces pulse_orb.dart
  • Wide display type, one width setting inside BrandText
  • Home with the orbit of shields and the Today strip
  • Four tabs with no drawer, and the shield detail screen
  • The check as segments, with results that count only what ran
  • An adaptive shell: two panes on foldables and tablets, a sidebar on desktop
  • Desktop extras: command palette, menu bar popover, window chrome
  • Mark a breach as handled, so the ring can close
  • Widgets, the share card and a family or devices screen

To wire or fix

  • The breach and hidden VPN probes, so those shields can report
  • Three score signals that return nothing today
  • The iPhone results line, so it stops counting 11
  • One vocabulary: shields, and the verb check
  • A copy sweep of the 166 em dash lines
  • The Home headline back in ink, so one lime action leads
